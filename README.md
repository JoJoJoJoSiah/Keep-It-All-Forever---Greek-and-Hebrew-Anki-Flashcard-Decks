# Keep Greek and Hebrew for Life!

Ready to make your memory of Greek and Hebrew a choice instead of leaving it up to chance? These flashcards, when done every day, will keep your load light and easy (without overwhelm) so that you can learn and retain your knowledge of God's word for the rest of your life. You'll get to "keep it all forever"!

These flashcard packs include the alphabet, vocab words, and grammar for Greek and Hebrew. Virtually every one of the vocab words in the Greek and Hebrew packs has an image and mnemonic device along with it to greatly aid in your memory.

**To start using the flashcards, see the "How to Use" section below.**

# How to Download the Flashcards

*To download the flashcards, simply click on each pack you'd like to have in the section above, click on it's title once more, and then click the download button* (If you're on mobile, you may have to click on the three dot menu to see the download button.).

# How to Use

Feel free to get going by reading the instructions below or watching [this video tutorial](https://youtu.be/5Bxp3ht9HXM?si=0q0XiLRROK-76c9). However, if you watch the video to get yourself set up, make sure to take a quick look at the little notes at the bottom of this document with the asterisk.

1. First, download the Anki app on your device. Find the appropriate download link for your computer or phone at the bottom of this webpage: https://apps.ankiweb.net/ (Note: The application on iPhone costs money, but you can use it in your phone browser for free if you upload the packs from a different device and just sync your decks online.)
2. Download the files in the OneDrive folder for the Greek and Hebrew flashcards.
3. Open Anki and find the "import" button.
4. In the little file explorer pop up, find the decks and import each of them, just using the default settings.
5. Click on the settings cog next to the top deck for Greek or Hebrew (or on mobile, hold down on the deck) and click "Deck options."
6. Here you can adjust the amount of new cards it will give you per day. There are two cards per word, so if you leave it at 20 it will give you 10 new words per day.
7. Scroll down and turn on FSRS. This improves the algorithm for the flashcard reviews. If you enable this on the top deck, it will enable it for all of the decks below it.
8. If you scroll down, there is a section where you can make one of the days of the week more easy. So you could make Saturday or Sunday have an easier workload.
9. After you have finished those things for Greek, go and do it for the top deck for Hebrew too (or the other way around, it doesn't matter which one you do first).
10. If you want to add the Greek and Hebrew alphabet packs, you can go through that same process with those (Note: When choosing a pronunciation style for the Greek alphabet, it might be worth knowing that the English mnemonic memory sentences in the Greek
flashcards generally use the modern pronunciation. However, most of the time, these mnemonic sentences also work for the Erasmian Greek pronunciation.).
11. You can now go and try it out either deck, just click on the top deck and complete that every day. If you want to, you can also try individual decks below, but doing just the top deck every day will give you all the cards in the entire deck, and will make it much more
manageable. An exception to this would be if you are preparing for a test for a specific chapter, then you can go through and complete the deck for that chapter before your quiz.
12. You can repeat this same process with the Grammar and Alphabet decks.

*Notes: You can simply enable the FSRS setting to the very top deck and it will automatically apply it to all of the decks under it in the drop down menu. Also, if you are preparing for a specific test on a chapter of the Van/Pratico Hebrew book, you can simply use the chapter drop down menu in the Hebrew deck and just complete the specific vocab words for that chapter a week or two before your test is due, and you will be golden!
