# Keep Greek and Hebrew for Life!

Ready to make your memory of Greek and Hebrew a choice instead of leaving it up to chance? These flashcards, when done every day, will keep your load light and easy (without overwhelm) so that you can learn and retain your knowledge of God's Word for the rest of your life. You'll get to "keep it all forever"!

These flashcard packs include the alphabet, vocabulary words, and grammar for Greek and Hebrew. Virtually every one of the vocabulary words in the Greek and Hebrew packs has an image and mnemonic device along with it to greatly aid in your memory. The 1,100 most common Biblical Greek and 641 most common Biblical Hebrew words are included. Knowing this many words allows you to read around 90-95% of the New Testament, and 80%-90% of the Old Testament.

**To start using the flashcards, see the "How to Use the Flashcards" section below.**

# How to Download the Flashcards

To download all the flashcards at once in a zip file, simply click the download button on [this page](Zip_File_Containing_All_Flashcard_Packs/All_Greek_and_Hebrew_Anki_Flashcard Decks_(v1.9).zip). If you are on mobile, you may have to click on the three-dot menu to see the download button.

If you'd like to download any individual packs, you can click through the menus in the section above to do so. Simply click on any pack you'd like, click its title once again, and then click the download button.

# How to Use the Flashcards

Feel free to get going by reading the instructions below or watching [this video tutorial](https://youtu.be/5Bxp3ht9HXM?si=0q0XiLRROK-76c9). However, if you watch the video to get yourself set up, make sure to take a quick look at the notes marked with an asterisk at the bottom of this document.

1. First, download the Anki app on your device. Find the appropriate download link for your computer or phone at the bottom of this webpage: https://apps.ankiweb.net/ (Note: The application on iPhone costs money, but you can use it in your phone browser for free if you upload the packs from a different device and just sync your decks online.)
2. Download the Greek and Hebrew flashcard packs you'd like to use.
3. Open Anki and find the "import" button.
4. In the little file explorer pop-up, find the decks and import each of them, just using the default settings.
5. Click on the settings cog next to the top deck for Greek or Hebrew (or on mobile, hold down on the deck) and click "Deck options."
6. Here you can adjust the amount of new cards it will give you per day. There are two cards per word, so if you leave it at 20 it will give you 10 new words per day.
7. Scroll down and turn on "FSRS." This improves the algorithm for the flashcard reviews. If you enable this on the top deck, it will enable it for all of the decks below it.
8. If you scroll further down, there is a section where you can make one of the days of the week easier. So, you could make Saturday or Sunday have an easier workload, for example.
9. Repeat steps 5-8 for the top deck of any other set of flashcards you'll be using.
10. If you haven't already added the Greek and Hebrew alphabet packs, and you'd like to have them, you can go through that same process with those (Note: When choosing a pronunciation style for the Greek alphabet, it might be worth knowing that the English mnemonic memory sentences in the Greek flashcards generally use the modern pronunciation. However, most of the time, these mnemonic sentences also work for the Erasmian Greek pronunciation.).
11. You can now try out any of those decks. Simply click on the vocab or grammar section and complete that every day (or the alphabet, if you're just getting started with the Greek/Hebrew). If you want to, you can also complete the individual sub-decks below those, but doing just the top decks every day will give you all the cards in the entire deck, and doing this will make it much more manageable. An exception to this would be if you are preparing for a test for a specific chapter, then you can go through and complete the deck for that chapter before your quiz.

*Notes: You can simply enable the FSRS setting on the very top deck and it will automatically apply it to all of the decks under it in the drop-down menu. Also, if you are preparing for a specific test on a chapter of the Van/Pratico Hebrew book, you can simply use the chapter drop-down menu in the Hebrew deck and just complete the specific vocab words for that chapter a week or two before your test is due, and you will be golden!
